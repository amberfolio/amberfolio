# Commercial licensing

Amber Folio is available to everyone under **AGPL-3.0-only**, which is
free for any use — including commercial use — as long as its terms are
honored. In broad strokes: modified versions that are conveyed, or that
users interact with over a network, must make their source available
under the same license — but the [license text](LICENSE) itself, not
this summary, defines the boundary.

If your use case can't work with the AGPL — embedding the emulator in a
closed-source product, distribution through a store whose terms conflict
with the AGPL, or a proprietary fork or service — a commercial license
exception is available from the maintainer.

A commercial exception covers only the rights the maintainer controls —
the Amber Folio code itself. Third-party dependencies remain under their
own licenses.
