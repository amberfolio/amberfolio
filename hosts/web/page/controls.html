Amber Folio Controls

Every keyboard improvement in the build, by where you are in Pool of Radiance. The game's own keys still work everywhere. The tag is the one enhancement you switch on; untagged rows are the game's own or always on.

Desktop: right-click for the seam panel, right-click again for the key card. Web: the seam list and the key card sit under the game.
modern-controls

One switch covers every row below that carries this tag: the arrows in lists, Enter, Esc and Left and Right at the bars, the main menu's cursor, the number row's heroes at the main menu, walking as a mode on the exploring bar, text entry that ignores the arrows, and the selection drawn as a yellow block.

Combat, Modify and text entry keep the game's own keys.

What these change

  • Right at the post-combat Take bar no longer takes Money, and Left at the temple's keep prompt no longer keeps. Typing M or K still does.
  • At camp, Magic and Alter, Left and Right no longer send the selection back to the first hero.
  • In the rest menu, Left and Right step the highlight; Y, H and M still pick days, hours or minutes.
  • With modern-controls on, Area is gone from the exploring bar (the automap is the overhead view), and the arrows walk only after Move.
  • The number row picks a hero at the main menu only; everywhere else it does what the keypad does.
