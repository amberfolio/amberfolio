scanned proclamation 59 491
Proclamation the fifty-ninth, and every word of it belongs to this
repository. It is here so that a citation has something to open,
and so that no character of anybody's own booklet is committed to
this tree in order to prove that opening one works.

The council of this invented city declares that its walls shall be
walked at dusk, that its gates shall be shut at dark, and that a
party returning after either shall wait until morning like anybody
else. Signed, a clerk who does not exist.
