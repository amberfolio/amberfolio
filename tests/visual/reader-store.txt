Entry three, and every word of it belongs to this repository.
It exists so that a leg has something to open, page and put away
without a single character of anybody's booklet being committed here.

The paragraph below is long on purpose. A page of the reader holds
twelve rows of twenty-two cells, so a note of a few hundred bytes
becomes several pages, and several pages are what the paging leg
needs in order to have anything to turn.

Wrapping happens at a space, so a run of ordinary words is what
proves it: alpha bravo charlie delta echo foxtrot golf hotel india
juliett kilo lima mike november oscar papa quebec romeo sierra
tango uniform victor whiskey xray yankee zulu, and round again,
alpha bravo charlie delta echo foxtrot golf hotel india juliett.

The last line of the last page is here so that a leg can tell the
end of the note from the end of a page.
